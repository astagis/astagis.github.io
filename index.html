Mikki

A personal assistant for one person

Mikki is a private, self-hosted assistant used only by its owner, Ásta Ægisdóttir. It is not a public service and does not accept other users.

With the owner's permission, Mikki reads and writes the owner's own Google Calendar and Google Sheets. It uses this to log expenses and help keep track of the day.
