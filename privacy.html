Privacy policy

Mikki · last updated 8 October 2026

Mikki is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Ásta Ægisdóttir. No one else can sign in to it or use it.

What data Mikki accesses

Only the owner's own Google account, and only these two services:

  • Google Calendar: to read events and, when the owner asks, add events.
  • Google Sheets: to read and add rows in the owner's own expense spreadsheet.

Mikki does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests. The access token is stored on the owner's private server. To answer requests, the content of a request may be processed by the AI model provider the owner has chosen (Anthropic).

Data is never sold, shared with third parties for any other purpose, or used for advertising.

Mikki's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
